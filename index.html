Slopfest Bingo Ruleset

Spot the prompts on your bingo board and mark them off as the action unfolds.

How to play

  1. Deal a card. Tap “Generate board” to get a fresh 5×5 board. The middle square is free.
  2. Spot it. Mark it. Tap a square whenever you see its prompt happen. Tap again to unmark it.
  3. Get bingo! Complete a row, column, or diagonal. Keep going and mark every square for a blackout.
